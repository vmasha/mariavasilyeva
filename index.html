About

Across scales, from local structure to global behavior.

I am an Assistant Professor in Applied Mathematics in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi.

My research sits at the intersection of numerical analysis, multiscale model reduction, and scientific machine learning. I am especially interested in reliable algorithms for multiscale media, coupled multiphysics processes, graph-based systems, learning on local approximation spaces, and reduced-order representation.
